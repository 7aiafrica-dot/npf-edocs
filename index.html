Which election, and who should handle it?

Presidential matters go to Force Headquarters by default. All others go to the State Command.

This service is for electoral offences only, such as vote buying, ballot snatching or violence. Challenges to election results go to the Election Petition Tribunals, and civil disputes to the courts.
