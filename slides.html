How it works

From the citizen's phone to an outcome, on record

1

Submit

Election, offence, polling unit, evidence. Phone verified by OTP.

2

Pay

One official ₦20,000 fee through a licensed gateway.

3

Slip

Serial number, QR code and receipt, also by SMS and email.

4

Deliver

Signed hard copy to the named desk within 72 hours.

5

Log

Registry scans the number. The clock starts.

6

Investigate

Commander minutes to a unit. Every move recorded.

7

Outcome

Sent to INEC, charged to court, or closed with reasons.

Two levels, kept apart: petitions go to Force Headquarters (federal) or a State Command (state). Neither can see the other's files.

The serial number

One number that every desk can check, even offline

Issued only after payment

The gateway must confirm on our server first. A redirect is never enough.

Check digit

The last digit catches typos and invented numbers at any desk, with no network.

Routed by design

Level and state are in the number, so a petition for another command is turned away.

Security and data protection

Hosted in Nigeria. Owned by the Force.

Compliant with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, with an impact assessment, privacy notice and a named data protection officer.

  • Encrypted in transit and at rest, with daily backups and tested restores.
  • Evidence files scanned for malware and served only through signed, logged links.
  • NIN optional. Only the verification result is kept.
  • 99.9% availability target around the election windows.
  • Independent penetration test before going live.
